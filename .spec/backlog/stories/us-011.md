# Confirm the exact proposed operation

As an authorized caller, I can inspect a proposed write and explicitly approve or reject it before any business mutation.

## Acceptance criteria

- An approval is bound to the application, owner, tenant, tool revision, saved arguments, and expiry.
- The confirmation endpoint claims the action atomically, rechecks policy, and rejects conflicting or stale requests.
