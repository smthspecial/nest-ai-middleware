# Complete a bounded multi-tool read workflow

As an authorized caller, I can ask a question that needs several business reads and receive a final answer.

The model sees authorized tools and can use ordered results from earlier rounds. The loop validates complete batches and enforces time, model-round, and tool-call limits. The packed consumer verifies a multi-tool request.

Complete dependent-round and limit coverage in [TASK-009](../tasks/task-009.md). Opt-in parallel reads are tracked in [TASK-025](../tasks/task-025.md).
