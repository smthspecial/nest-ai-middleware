# Continue a stored conversation

As a verified conversation owner, I can resume a conversation after restart when the host enables history.

## Acceptance criteria

- History is opt-in and independently retained. Access requires the trusted application, tenant, and owner.
- Expected-revision appends protect conversation order and preserve a bounded transcript.
