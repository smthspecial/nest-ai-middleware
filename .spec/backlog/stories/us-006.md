# Store bookkeeping in PostgreSQL

As an operator, I can inject a persistent bookkeeping store and apply documented migrations and retention.

## Acceptance criteria

- The optional adapter works with the host's existing database pool and preserves records across restart.
- Repeated storage operations are idempotent. Reports and cleanup are scoped by application, and expiry cascades to event rows.
